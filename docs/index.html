Quick start#

Try without installing#

ZDOTDIR=/path/to/omarchy-tweaks/dotfiles/zsh zsh -i
# or, with ~/.local/bin on PATH:
zsh-dev

Install#

ln -sf /path/to/omarchy-tweaks/dotfiles/zsh/.zshrc ~/.zshrc

Symlink, don't copy — the loader resolves lib/ relative to its own location. Full details in dotfiles/zsh/README.md.
